Ways to engage

Engagement models

Same quality of thinking—different cadence and commercial shape. We’ll recommend one after a short diagnostic.

01

Advisory retainer

Standing access to a named partner and specialist bench. Best for ongoing decisions, quarterly planning, and light-touch steering across workstreams.

02

Fixed sprint

Time-boxed outcomes—e.g. “GTM reset in eight weeks” or “forecast model in six.” Clear deliverables, fixed fee where scope allows.

03

Program / transformation

Multi-quarter initiatives with embedded leads, steering committees, and milestone billing. Suited to ERP rollouts and enterprise-wide change.
